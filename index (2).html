We believe that creativity grows through collaboration, curiosity and the exchange of ideas. KindDesign brings together people from different creative backgrounds to learn from each other, build connections and explore new perspectives.

Who are we?

KindDesign was founded by Julia and Jeremy, who shared an interest in design and creativity. Inspired by Julia's solo trip to Lisbon, where she experienced the need for a space to meet like-minded creatives, they created a community in Copenhagen where people can connect, share ideas and inspire one another.

How can we collaborate?

Share your knowledge

Are you a designer, artist or creative professional with something to share? Host a talk, workshop or presentation and inspire others with your experience, ideas and expertise.

Offer a space

Have a studio, workspace or venue that could host a creative gathering? Help us create a space where people can meet, exchange ideas and get inspired.

Host an event

Have an idea for a creative meetup, workshop or event? We'd love to collaborate and bring your idea to life by connecting you with our creative community.

Let's create something together.

Have an idea? Let's talk.

Whether you have an idea for a workshop, want to share your knowledge, offer a space, volunteer your time or simply have an idea you'd love to bring to life, we'd love to hear from you. There's no idea too small, and everyone has something to contribute to our community. Let's make something meaningful together.
